A chat with Emily

Join us in this episode of the podcast where we chat with Emily from the We Belong community.

This introduces Emily, a long-term employee at the Flagstaff Group, who shares her personal experiences as part of the “We Belong” podcast series.

She describes her neurodivergence using the metaphor of a high-speed racetrack and explains how anxiety and epilepsy impact her daily life. Emily reflects on the importance of her official diagnosis at age fifteen, which provided her with much-needed clarity regarding her invisible challenges.

She often has to ask the public for patience and understanding. She also highlights the profound emotional support her recent marriage provides, which has helped her overcome feelings of isolation.

The Flagstaff Group is an organisation dedicated to fostering community inclusion and providing life skills for people with disabilities. This dialogue serves to humanise the lived experience of disability while advocating for a more supportive and inclusive society.
