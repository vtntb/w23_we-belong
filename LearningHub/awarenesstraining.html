Disability Inclusion

Course Objectives

  • Foster access and inclusion of people with disability as customers and employees
  • Understand the barriers to inclusion that people with disability experience
  • Foster communication and interaction with people with disability
  • Learn about disabilities

Disability Awareness Outcome

At the end of the session you will:

  • Learn about some types of disabilities
  • Learn from our people with a disability about some of their customer experiences
  • Understand why work is important for people with disability
  • Learn about appropriate language, behaviours & etiquette

Lesson 2 – Types of Disability
Lesson 3 – Lived Experience Video
