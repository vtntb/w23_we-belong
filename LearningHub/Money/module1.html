What is a budget?

  • Something that shows you how much money you receive and how much you can spend.
  • A plan for how you will spend your money each week, fortnight, month or year.
  • A way to help you work towards future money goals.
  • A way in which you can keep track of where your money is going and how to start saving it.

Why is budgeting important?

  • It keeps your money organised
  • Make sure you don’t just SPEND the money you earn
  • You can save towards small goals
  • You can save towards a larger goal

Income

Income = money received for work or through investments

Types of income are:

  • Wages (i.e. salary)
  • Investments
  • Interest
  • Selling something you create or own

For most people salary is wages received on a regular basis from work

Expenses

Expenses = the cost required to obtain a product or service.

Types of expenses are:

  • Rent
  • Mortgage
  • Food
  • Bills

Can you think about some of your daily expenses?

Lets look at an example...

Suzie is 19 and is living at home. She receives $191.80 a week (income). She has the following expenses:

Board to her parents $30.00
Mobile bill $15.00
Clothes $20.00
Going out twice with friends $30.00
Public transport to TAFE twice a week $5.00
Coffee $4.00
Lunches while at TAFE $17.95
Snacks $4.80
Total $126.75

How can Suzie save more?

At the end of the week Suzie has $65.05 left ($191.80 minus $126.75).

To save more Suzie can:

  • Buy lunch once a week and bring lunch from home on other days
  • Purchase clothing once a month not weekly
  • Bring friends home rather than going out all the time
  • Buy snacks from the supermarket

What are needs and wants?

Needs = the basic things that people must have in order to survive. For example: water, shelter, food.

Wants = are the things that make life more interesting and fun. In other words, you could live without them if you had to.

A budget should cover your Needs and help you achieve your Wants.
