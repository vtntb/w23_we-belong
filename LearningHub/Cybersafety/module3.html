How to keep yourself safe online

  • Don’t post personal information online
  • Think carefully about posting pictures or videos of yourself online
  • Keep your privacy settings as high as possible
  • Never give out your passwords
  • Don’t befriend people online you don’t know
  • Don’t meet up with people you have met online
  • Remember that not everyone online is who they say they are

Other cybersafety tips

  • Check the privacy settings on your mobile and social media
  • Clear your browsing history regularly
  • Don’t open pop-ups or spam emails EVER
  • Don’t send rude pics on your phone
  • Never give out your address or mobile number online
  • Don’t share your passwords
  • Be careful of people who are not real friends online
  • Learn how to block people on your phone or on Facebook
  • Turn your mobile phone off every now and then
  • Get help if you get rude or pushy messages from strangers wanting to meet up
  • Check if any strange apps have been added to your phone by someone else, tracking or stalking apps

Smartphone Rules

  • Lock your keypad
  • Delete browsing history
  • Don’t post unkind messages about friends or other people
  • Don’t exchange nude, inappropriate nor embarrassing pictures
  • Respect others, do not post photos of people without their knowledge
  • Don’t take photos in changing rooms, bathrooms or pool toilets
  • Don’t share other people’s photos especially rude ones
