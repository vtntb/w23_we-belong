Disability Inclusive Employment

People with a disability take less sick days, have higher retention rates and are rated more productive.

When employee health and wellness is managed well, their engagement increases from 7% to 55%.

People with a disability in employment:

  • 37% are professionals, managers and administrators
  • 30% are clerical sales and service workers
  • 33% are tradespersons, production, and transport workers as well as labourers and related workers

The Benefits of Disability Inclusive Employment

  • Increased employee engagement
  • Positive organisational reputation
  • Increased customer base
  • Possible subsidies and support
