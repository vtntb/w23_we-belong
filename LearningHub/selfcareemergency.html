Course Objective

This comprehensive course on Self-care During Emergencies aims to equip you with essential skills and knowledge to identify problems, manage emotions and get support during a disaster.

Topic covered in this course:

  • What an emergency is
  • How to remain calm
  • Signs you need extra support
  • Support services and resources

Course Content

Module 1: What Is An Emergency?
Module 2: How To Remain Calm During An Emergency
Module 3: Signs That I Need Extra Help or Support
Module 4: Support Services & Resources
