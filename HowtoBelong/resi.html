What is Resilience?

Resilience means not giving up when life feels hard. It means keeping going, learning, and becoming stronger over time.

No one is born resilient. It is something we can build through support, practice, and positive experiences.

Resilience helps people feel more confident, handle challenges, and keep moving forward.

Inner Strength

Resilience helps you stay calm, keep trying, and believe in yourself.

Personal Growth

Every challenge can teach you something and help you grow stronger.

What Can Build Resilience?

Positive Emotions

Good feelings can help you feel stronger, even when life is difficult.

Relationships

Friends, family, and kind people around you can help you feel supported.

Achievement

Small wins matter. Reaching goals can build confidence and motivation.
