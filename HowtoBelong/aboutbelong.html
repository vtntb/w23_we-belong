About the Program

What is We Belong?

We Belong is a project that supports people with disability to feel included, confident, and connected in the community.

It encourages people to take part in sport, hobbies, volunteering, and social activities, while also helping communities become more welcoming and inclusive.

Why We Belong Matters

Many people with disability face barriers when trying to join community activities. We Belong helps reduce those barriers through support, information, and inclusion.

Support Participation

The project helps people explore activities and feel more prepared to join community life.

Build Confidence

It encourages people to believe in themselves and try new experiences with greater confidence.

Create Inclusion

It also supports clubs, coaches, and communities to become more respectful, accessible, and welcoming.

Who can get involved with We Belong?

  • People with a disability
  • Sporting programs
  • Community groups
  • Local schools, colleges, TAFE’s or Universities
  • Health and Fitness Industry
  • Youth programs, people aged over 16
  • Hobby Groups
  • Service Organisations
  • Not for Profits

Top tips for creating a welcoming and inclusive environment

  1. See the person, not their disability.
  2. Treat everyone with respect, dignity and equality.
  3. Speak directly to the person and maintain eye contact.
  4. Refer to the person first, not their disability. Reflect on their individuality and humanity, not their condition.
  5. Choose inclusive language and avoid stereotypes or labels that diminish a person.

How We Help

  • Sharing real stories and lived experiences
  • Providing guidance for joining community activities
  • Promoting confidence, resilience, and belonging
  • Encouraging inclusive practice in the community

A Place to Belong

We Belong is not only about joining an activity. It is about feeling accepted, respected, and valued. The project helps people find connection and reminds them that they have a place in the community.
