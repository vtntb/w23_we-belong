Coaching Tools

Coaches and support workers can use this checklist to prepare for inclusive sessions. It covers communication, environment, participation, and safety considerations to help create welcoming spaces for people with disabilities.

The checklist is designed to be quick and easy to use, helping coaches ensure their session is accessible and inclusive.

Inclusion is an ongoing process. Use this checklist as a starting point, but always be open to feedback and ready to adapt based on the needs of the young people you work with.

By taking these steps, coaches and teaching staff can help create a positive experience where every individuals feels valued and supported.
