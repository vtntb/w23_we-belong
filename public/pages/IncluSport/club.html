A Sports Club can have many teams who play different sports. They can be small with local teams or large with professional teams. Sports Clubs will provide a facility and equipment for training and competition. Sports Clubs are organised by volunteers and are a great place to make new friends. This creates a community who value health and wellbeing.

To be an inclusive Sports Club, read How to be an Inclusive Sports Club.
