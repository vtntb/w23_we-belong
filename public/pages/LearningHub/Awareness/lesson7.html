Consider your organisations:

  • Marketing/Communications: Print, TV, Website, Direct Podcast, Phone, Apps, Captioning, and Plain English Versions
  • Commitment: Directors, CEO, Managers, Staff, Suppliers, Partners
  • Products/Services: Innovation, Packaging, Technology, NDIS
  • Infrastructure: Location, Transport Links, Business Hours, Premises, Parking, Access, Restrooms

When reviewing materials your organisation produces why not consider the following:

  • Easy Read Collateral – Create alternate versions of collateral to increase the level understanding among your customers and the wider community.
  • Website Accessibility – websites should comply with the latest Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. Full text transcripts for audio and video, closed captioning and tracking elements to improve audio descriptions are required (i.e. explaining silences in text, describe who is speaking… etc).

Communication Boards – What Are They?

Communication boards are devices that allow people with limited language skills to express their needs, preferences, and decisions by selecting images that represent words.

A communication board is a device that displays photos, symbols, or illustrations to help people with limited language skills express themselves. The user can gesture, point to, or blink at images to communicate with others. They can be simple, handmade boards or apps and computer programs people can use on smartphones, tablets, or laptops.

Many people may benefit from communication boards, such as autistic people, people recovering from stroke or brain injury, and people with ALS and learning disabilities.
