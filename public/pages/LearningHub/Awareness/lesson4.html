Learning Hub > Disability Awareness Training > Lesson 4 – Customer Service Experiences

Of the Australians with disability aged 15 and over, almost one-third (33.1%) avoided community situations because of their disability. One of the most common situations avoided included going to shops, banks and community places. (34.3%)

1 in 3 people with a disability report that their customer needs are often unmet.

36% of people with disability are often treated less favorably than customers without disability.

People with disability are three times as likely to avoid an organisation and twice as likely to dissuade others because of an organisation’s negative inclusion reputation.
