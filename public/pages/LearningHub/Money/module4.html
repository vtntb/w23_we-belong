Moving out of home

Moving out of home for the first time can be exciting and a little scary. Before you make the move you need to be aware of the following costs.

  • Upfront bond payment
  • Regular weekly payments
  • Electricity, gas, water and other bills
  • Groceries
  • Personal expenses – mobile bill, clothes, travel, entertainment, etc.

What will you take with you?

Whether you’re moving out by yourself or with other people, you will need to make sure you have a:

  • Bed
  • Fridge/freezer
  • Table and chairs
  • Lounge and TV

How much will I need for bond?

Bond is a sum of money requested from tenants on behalf of building owners or real estate agents.

Real estate agents generally ask for a bond amount of 4 weeks rent plus 2 weeks payment in advance.

Example: If a flat or house is $200 a week to rent, the bond will be:

Bond = 4 x $200 = $800

Two weeks rent = 2 x $200 = $400

Total to pay: $800 + $400 = $1200

Ongoing costs

While living out of home you will need to save money for:

  • Rent – work out how much you have to pay each week.
  • Bills – write down when bills are due to avoid unnecessary surprises.
  • Groceries – look at meal plans and online shopping to help you stick to a budget.
  • Having fun – make sure you spend a little bit on yourself.

Finding a place

If you’re not sure what’s available to rent or if you’re looking for a flat mate, visit these websites:
