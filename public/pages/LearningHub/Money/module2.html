What’s a pay slip?

Pay slip = a note given to an employee when they have been paid, detailing the amount of pay given and the tax deducted.

Pay slips are usually emailed or handed to employees when pays have been made. This can occur weekly, fortnightly or monthly depending on how often the employer pays.

What information is on a pay slip?

  • Employers name
  • Your name
  • The pay period (i.e. week, fortnight or month)
  • Hourly rate you have been paid
  • Gross and net amount you have been paid
  • Tax deducted (PAYG)
  • Superannuation payments
  • Any accrued leave you have

What’s a Tax File Number (TFN)?

Every Australian worker requires a tax file number when they first start work.

Your TFN is an important part of your tax and superannuation records.

You must apply to the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) to get your TFN.

It’s free to apply at

http://www.ato.gov.au/

Once you are issued a TFN it’s yours for life, regardless of whether you change jobs or move countries.

Why is tax deducted from my pay?
