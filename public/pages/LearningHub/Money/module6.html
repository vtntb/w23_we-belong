What’s a loan?

Money that is borrowed which is expected to be paid back with interest.

Before you get a loan

The things to consider before applying for a loan are:

  1. Can I pay it back?
  2. How long will it take me to pay it back?
  3. Will the reason I have the loan still be important in 5 years time?
  4. Can I source the money from elsewhere?
  5. Is the bank going to giving me a fair deal?

Applying for a loan

The credit provider may look at your credit report and ask for:

  • Payslips
  • Bank account statements
  • Copies of other credit contracts or bills
  • This is so they can verify your ability to meet the loan repayments without financial hardship.

Affordability and Repayments

What will my monthly payment be, and can I afford them?

Make sure you only borrow what you can afford to repay.

Investigate how to set up a direct debit from your bank account to make repayments.

Interest

Interest is money you owe on top of the amount borrowed.

The higher the interest rate the higher the amount owing.
