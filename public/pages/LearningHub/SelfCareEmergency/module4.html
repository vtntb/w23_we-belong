Self-Help Resources

  • www.headtohealth.gov.au – The Head to Health National Digital Mental Health Gateway. Provides easy access to digital mental health services, information and support.
  • Online Treatment Programs such as This Way Up
  • Apps – such as the Calm App, Headspace App, Smiling Mind, MindShift, ReachOut

*Please keep in mind that not all online programs and apps are of equal quality. It’s crucial to verify the evidence-based nature of any online resource you discover and plan to use. Additionally, consult with your support team to determine which ones are most suitable for your needs. The ones we have listed are all backed by evidence.
