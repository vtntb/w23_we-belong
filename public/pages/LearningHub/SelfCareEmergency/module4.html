Self-Help Resources

  • www.headtohealth.gov.au – The Head to Health National Digital Mental Health Gateway provides easy access to digital mental health services, information and support.
  • Online treatment programs such as This Way Up.
  • Apps such as Calm, Headspace, Smiling Mind, MindShift and ReachOut.

Please keep in mind that not all online programs and apps are of equal quality. It’s important to check whether an online resource is evidence-based before using it. You can also speak with your support team to decide which resources are most suitable for your needs. The resources listed here are backed by evidence.
