What is Communication?

Communication is sending and receiving information from one place to another.

Forms of Communication

  • Verbal – Speech, sounds, language, tone of voice
  • Non-verbal – Facial expression and body language
  • Written – Emails, text messages, letters, articles
  • Visual – Signs, symbols, pictures, images
  • Oral – listening and hearing

What is Effective Communication?

Effective communication is the successful transfer of information between people.

It enables problem solving, decision making, improves relationships and teamwork.

Difficult messages if communicated well and respectfully can avoid conflict, help with self-awareness and improve productivity.

To be an good communicator, you need to be an active listener

Active listening is:

When you are completely focused on what the other person is saying

When you are hearing everything the other person is saying

Tips to being a good active listener

  • Have good body language.
  • Face the speaker, maintain eye contact
  • Be present. Pay attention and relax
  • Screen out distractions such as background noise.
  • Keep an open mind.
  • Allow the speaker to speak without interruption, listen without jumping to conclusions.
  • Listen and picture what the speaker is saying. The speaker may be asking a question, listen to the key points the speaker is asking for.
  • Wait for the speaker to pause, ask for clarification. Repeat what the speaker has said back to him/her will ensure you have heard the information correctly.
