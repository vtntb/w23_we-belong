What are examples of Verbal Communication?

  • Speaking face to face
  • On your phone
  • Email
  • Social media – Facetime
  • Zoom

What are some examples of Non-Verbal communication?

  • Body language
  • Gestures
  • Facial expressions
  • Body posture
  • Speech
  • Eye movement
  • Dress and appearance

It is important that when we are communicating in the workplace and don’t ask inappropriate questions about:

  • Family problems at home
  • Relationship problems
  • Try and give relationship advice
  • Religion
  • Health concerns
  • Politics

Always think before you speak

  • If it isn’t nice to say don’t say it at all
  • Always pay attention and listen if someone is speaking to you
  • Make and maintain eye contact
  • Use the correct body language
  • don’t hold grudges in the workplace you don’t want there to be any conflict, it can take a toll on the way people work

Conflict

Why is it important that we keep conflict away from the workplace?

  • Be aware that in some point in the workplace that there are going to be some sort of a misunderstanding and this is why we have to use our communication to work it out
  • It is important to keep conflict away so we can work to the best of our ability at work.

Resolving Conflict

  • Make sure that you resolve the issue at work so that it doesn’t because a bigger matter than it should be.
  • Control your emotion and behaviour in the situation
  • Pay attention to the feelings that are being expressed as well as the words spoken by other
  • Respect the differences of the other people
  • Don’t continue the conflict once you have communicated and fixed the problem
