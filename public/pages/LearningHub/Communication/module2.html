What are examples of Verbal Communication?

  • Speaking face to face
  • On your phone
  • Email
  • Social media – Facetime
  • Zoom

What are some examples of Non-Verbal Communication?

  • Body language
  • Gestures
  • Facial expressions
  • Body posture
  • Speech
  • Eye movement
  • Dress and appearance

It is important that when we are communicating in the workplace, we don’t ask inappropriate questions about:

  • Family problems at home
  • Relationship problems
  • Relationship advice
  • Religion
  • Health concerns
  • Politics

Always Think Before You Speak

  • If it isn’t nice to say, don’t say it at all.
  • Always pay attention and listen if someone is speaking to you.
  • Make and maintain eye contact.
  • Use the correct body language.
  • Don’t hold grudges in the workplace. You don’t want conflict because it can affect how people work.

Conflict

Why is it important that we keep conflict away from the workplace?

  • Sometimes in the workplace there may be misunderstandings. This is why we need to use communication to work things out.
  • It is important to keep conflict away so we can work to the best of our ability.

Resolving Conflict

  • Make sure that you resolve the issue at work so that it doesn’t become a bigger matter than it should be.
  • Control your emotions and behaviour in the situation.
  • Pay attention to the feelings being expressed as well as the words spoken by others.
  • Respect the differences of other people.
  • Don’t continue the conflict once you have communicated and fixed the problem.
