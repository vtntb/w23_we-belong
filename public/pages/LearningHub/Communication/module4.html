Learning Hub > Communication > Module 4 – How to Come Up with Good Conversation Topics

Just as there is a way you should dress for work, there are also ways you should speak, and things you should and should not speak about

Let us explore some workplace etiquette that may help you when you start work.

Religion

  • You should not discuss your religious beliefs.
  • Do not talk about other religious beliefs at work.
  • Religion is a very personal issue and people are very sensitive about it.
  • People may not want to hear that you disagree with their religious beliefs.
  • Everyone should respect each other’s religion.

Politics

  • Politics can be a sensitive issue, particularly around election time.
  • While you may feel strongly about your political party or candidate, you should not try to win your co-workers over to your point of view.

Your Relationships

  • Your relationships at work will be different.
  • A supervisor or boss
  • A work colleague
  • A mentor
  • Friendships
  • People we respect
  • People who we don’t have anything to do with
  • People who you may not like

Be Careful Who You Tell

  • Your personal weekend activities
  • Too much drinking
  • Drug usage
  • Illegal activities

Knock Knock?

  • It may just be a joke to you, but co-workers can take great offense to off-colour humour that makes fun of race, religion, sex, or appearance.
  • If there is any question about whether your joke is appropriate for the workplace, save it for after-hours around trusted friends and family.

So, what is the appropriate way to speak in the workplace?

  • Your work colleagues will want to get to know a little about you.
  • Chatting about things like movies and TV shows is usually a safe conversation.
  • Sport is a good thing to talk about.

One Step at a Time

  • Learning how to converse is an art.
  • It is a skill that takes time to develop.
