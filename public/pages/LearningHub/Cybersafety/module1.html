What is cybersafety?

Cybersafety is ensuring your safety as well as that of others when using technology such as computers, tablets and smartphones especially whilst online or on the Internet.

This means:

  • Making sure your personal information is safeguarded
  • Learning what is good behaviour online such as when posting comments, replying to messages and the like.
  • Maintaining respect for others online. Practicing etiquette or “netiquette” as with the real world applied to the virtual world.
