What is Cybersafety?

Cybersafety is ensuring your safety as well as that of others when using technology such as computers, tablets and smartphones, especially while online or using the Internet.

This means:

  • Making sure your personal information is safeguarded.
  • Learning what is good behaviour online, such as when posting comments, replying to messages, and communicating with others.
  • Maintaining respect for others online. This means practising etiquette, or “netiquette”, in the virtual world just as you would in the real world.

What Makes the Internet Unsafe?

  • Cyberbullies
  • Internet trolls
  • Cyberstalkers or online predators
  • Cyberharassment
  • Impersonators
  • Online embarrassment
  • Fraud
  • Copyright infringement and plagiarism
  • Phishing
  • Computer viruses
  • Trojans, malware, and adware
  • Invasion of privacy
  • Fake emails
  • Hackers
  • Identity thieves
  • Inappropriate or mature content
  • In-app purchases
