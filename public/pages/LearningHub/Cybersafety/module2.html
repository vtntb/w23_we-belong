What can happen to me if I’m not cybersafe?

Emotional Effects – cyberharassment may cause lasting physiological and psychological effects like chronic fatigue, depression and a feeling of hopelessness and low self-esteem.

Other impacts – cyberbullying can result in violence, injury and even death.

Financial Impacts – cybercrime has been on the rise with devastating effects on individuals and families. It happens not only to individuals but companies as well.

Even the legitimate “In-app purchases” get parents by surprise when they are left with a bill when they lend their tablet or phone to their children.
