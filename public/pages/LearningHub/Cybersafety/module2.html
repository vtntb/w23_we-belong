What Can Happen to Me if I’m Not Cybersafe?

Emotional Effects – cyberharassment may cause lasting physiological and psychological effects like chronic fatigue, depression, feelings of hopelessness, and low self-esteem.

Other Impacts – cyberbullying can result in violence, injury, and even death.

Financial Impacts – cybercrime has been on the rise with devastating effects on individuals and families. It can happen to individuals and companies.

Even legitimate in-app purchases can surprise parents when they are left with a bill after lending their tablet or phone to their children.
