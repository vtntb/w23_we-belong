Guest Speaking

Our Inclusion Ambassadors can speak to groups about disability, inclusion, and the importance of making people feel welcome.

They share real life experiences and help others understand how simple actions can create a more supportive and inclusive community.

What they can speak about

  • ● Their own life experiences
  • ● Disability and inclusion
  • ● How to make people feel welcome and included

What you can learn

  • ● How to create a welcoming environment
  • ● How to support people with disability
  • ● Simple ways to include everyone

Meet Rebecca

Rebecca is one of our Inclusion Ambassadors. She is passionate about helping people feel connected and included in their community.

She shares her rehabilitation journey after having a stroke in her early 20s and speaks openly about her experiences.

Through workshops, social connection, and inclusive activities, Rebecca helps people build friendships and feel supported in everyday life.
