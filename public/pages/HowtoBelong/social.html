Being social is important because it helps us feel happy, safe and included. It matters because we can:

  • ● Make friendships
  • ● Helps feelings and emotions

Talking and spending time with people can help us to feel calm, happy and supported.

  • ● Helps learn skills

Being social helps us to practice talking, listening and sharing.

  • ● Helps people feel independent

Social activities help people make choices and try new things.

  • ● Helps people feel they belong

Being part of a group helps people feel accepted and cared for.

Being social helps people feel connected, confident and included.
