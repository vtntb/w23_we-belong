Confidence is all about believing in yourself and trusting that you can do what you want to do.

Confidence is a skill that we develop over time and with practice.

To grow our confidence, we must step outside of our comfort zones.

To build our confidence, we can:

  1. Positive Mindset: allows us to grow and improve how we think about ourselves and the world around us.
  2. Positive Self Talk: positively speak about yourself to boost confidence, motivation and resilience.
  3. Reflect on your progress.
  4. Practice self-compassion: treat yourself with kindness.
  5. Positive relationships: Good relationships help us feel happy, safe and supported.
  6. Embrace challenges.
  7. Recognise your achievement.
