A chat with Laurinda

Join us in this episode of the podcast where we chat with Laurinda from the We Belong community.

This podcast episode from The Flagstaff Group features an interview with Laurinda Martin, a woman who shares her journey of living and working with total blindness.

After a lengthy career in reception, she transitioned into a role helping others use assistive technology and Braille. Laurinda discusses how Toastmasters helped her build confidence in public speaking, using a specialised computer and memory techniques to prepare her presentations.

She advocates for inclusive employment, urging businesses to look past physical limitations and focus on an individual’s adaptability. The source highlights that with simple workplace adjustments, people with disabilities can contribute significantly to any professional organisation.
