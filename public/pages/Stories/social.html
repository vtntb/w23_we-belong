A social story is a visual guide or visual checklist that helps a person with disability to navigate how to participate in a specific activity.

Social stories have been proven useful when going to places such as airports, evacuation centres, etc.

When writing a social story, it is important to set the scene, describe the place in detail, use a character, simple language, and lots of pictures.
