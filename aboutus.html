Welcome to The Flagstaff Group

The Flagstaff Group is a not-for-profit disability enterprise and a registered NDIS provider that is dedicated to enhancing life skills and work opportunities for people with disability.

With 60 years of experience across the Illawarra, Macarthur and Shoalhaven region, Flagstaff’s integrated social enterprises create meaningful employment opportunities for over 250 people in an inclusive and supportive environment.

Our Vision

A world that only sees abilities.

Our Purpose

To provide life skills and work for people with a disability.
